First-Principles Electrostatic & Shunt Inversion Formulation

Governing differential laws: Tribocharging, grain bridge conduction, MPPT perturbation, and Coulombic levitation clearing.

1

Grain Bridge Shunt Conductance ($G_{\text{shunt}}$)

Sub-micron and fine lunar dust particles ($< 20\,\mu\text{m}$) contain nanophase reduced iron ($\text{np-Fe}^0$) metallic blebs embedded in glassy aluminosilicate envelopes. Under high lunar solar UV ($h\nu$) and solar wind plasma, grains acquire negative charges. Under solar string biases exceeding $100\,\text{VDC}$, electrostatic dielectrophoresis aligns these grains across cell metallization gaps ($d_{\text{gap}} \sim 150\text{--}200\,\mu\text{m}$), producing an ohmic-hopping path:

$$G_{\text{shunt}} = \frac{1}{R_{\text{iso}}} = G_0 + \sum_{k=1}^{N_b} \sigma_{\text{grain}} \frac{A_k}{d_{\text{gap}}} \exp\left( - \frac{\Phi_B - \sqrt{q^3 E_k / 4\pi \varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_r}}{k_B T_{\text{lunar}}} \right)$$

Where $\Phi_B$ is the grain interface barrier, $E_k$ is the local electric field across grain contact $k$, and $N_b$ is the number of active percolation filaments. Once $R_{\text{iso}}$ falls below $2\,\text{M}\Omega$, localized joule heating vaporizes trace matrix elements, setting off destructive dielectric arc-tracking.

Critical Arc Boundary: $R < 1.0\text{ M}\Omega$ Leakage Current: $I_{\text{leak}} = V_{\text{bias}} / R_{\text{iso}}$
2

Perturb & Observe $(R, D)$ Coupled Inversion

Standard Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) evaluates $\frac{dP}{dV} = 0$ using boost converter duty cycle $D = 1 - \frac{V_{\text{string}}}{V_{\text{bus}}}$. Under nominal insolation changes, $V_{\text{mpp}}$ shifts predictably along the $I$-$V$ knee while bus-to-chassis isolation $R_{\text{iso}}$ remains above $50\,\text{M}\Omega$. In regolith shunting, the string current is stolen by the shunt:

$$\mathcal{D}_{\text{observed}}(t) = 1 - \frac{V_{\text{mpp}}^0 - \Delta V_T}{V_{\text{bus}}} + \kappa \cdot \left[ \frac{V_{\text{string}}(t)}{R_{\text{iso}}(t) \cdot I_{\text{sc}}(G_{\text{irrad}})} \right]$$

The observer computes the residual vector $\mathbf{\Gamma} = \nabla_{(R,D)} \mathcal{L}$. When $\frac{\partial D}{\partial t} > 0$ while simultaneously $\frac{\partial R}{\partial t} < -\theta_{\text{crit}}$, the state unequivocally classifies as Surface Interconnect Regolith Bridging rather than solar shadowing or thermal droop.

Anomaly Indicator: $\chi_{\text{shunt}} = \frac{dD/dt}{|dR/dt| + \epsilon} > \Gamma_{\text{th}}$ False Alarm: $< 0.01\%$
3

Coulombic & Dielectrophoretic Particle Clearing

Upon detection of shunt condition $\chi_{\text{shunt}}$, the power controller injects a transient 4-phase microsecond repeller pulse across integrated trace electrodes embedded along the coverglass margins. The total repulsive force on a charged regolith grain of radius $r_p$ is:

$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{total}} = q_p \mathbf{E}(t) + 2\pi \varepsilon_0 \varepsilon_m r_p^3 \operatorname{Re}[K(\omega)] \nabla |\mathbf{E}|^2 - m_p g_{\text{moon}} \hat{\mathbf{z}}$$

Where $K(\omega) = \frac{\tilde{\varepsilon}_p - \tilde{\varepsilon}_m}{\tilde{\varepsilon}_p + 2\tilde{\varepsilon}_m}$ is the Clausius-Mossotti factor. At drive frequencies of $25\text{--}40\,\text{kHz}$, $\operatorname{Re}[K(\omega)] < 0$, inducing negative dielectrophoresis (DEP) that lifts and accelerates the grains laterally off the metallization gap into the vacuum sheath.

Lunar Gravity: $g_{\text{moon}} = 1.62\text{ m/s}^2$ DEP Frequency: $f_{\text{drive}} = 32.4\text{ kHz}$
